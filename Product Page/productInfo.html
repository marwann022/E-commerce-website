Frequently Asked Questions

Are they water-resistant?

The Aura Elite headphones feature an IPX4 rating, meaning they can withstand splashes and light rain, but should not be submerged.

Does the ANC work without playing music?

Yes, you can activate Active Noise Cancellation even when no audio is playing to enjoy a quiet environment.
